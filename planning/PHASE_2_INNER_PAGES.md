# PHASE 2: INNER PAGES DESIGN SYSTEM

## 1. General Layout Strategy (All Inner Pages)
Inner pages will forgo the heavy 3D WebGL experiences to prioritize fast loading, readable content, and straightforward navigation.
- **Header:** The global sticky header will load immediately in its solid Deep Navy state (`#0A192F`), unlike the transparent state on the homepage.
- **Page Hero:** Each inner page will feature a slim, highly polished hero section (approx `40vh`). 
  - Background: Deep Navy.
  - Content: Page Title (e.g., "Our Services") left-aligned, accompanied by a subtle breadcrumb trail (Home > Services).
  - Visual: A subtle, high-quality, darkened photographic background or abstract geometric pattern to maintain the premium feel without heavy rendering.
- **Content Container:** Standard centered `max-w-7xl` (1280px) container for readability.

## 2. About Us
- **Layout:** Alternating left/right text and image blocks to tell the company story.
- **Content Flow:**
  - **Introduction:** The evolution from Won Line and the upgrade of the shipping license.
  - **Vision & Outlook:** Highlight the goal to revolutionize the global supply chain, customer-centric solutions, and sustainability.
- **Visuals:** High-quality imagery of ports, ships, or abstract logistics concepts. If actual company photography is unavailable, use premium licensed stock that aligns with the color palette (heavy blues and natural light).
- **CTA:** "Explore Our Capabilities" pointing to the Services page.

## 3. Services (Hub Page)
- **Layout:** A grid or masonry layout listing the consolidated service categories.
- **Service Categories:**
  1. **Global Logistics & Forwarding:** (Combines Freight Forwarding, Door-to-Door, Transportation). Emphasis on TIR Carnets and Central Asian networks.
  2. **Agency Representation & Clearance:** (Combines Agency Rep, Customs Clearance). Emphasis on reliability, marketing dynamics, and compliance.
  3. **Cargo Handling & Warehousing:** (Combines Dry Boxes, Special Equipment, Warehousing). Emphasis on oversized cargo and international consolidation.
- **Card Design:** Large, interactive cards. On hover, the image scales slightly within its container, and an Ocean Blue gradient overlay appears with a "Read More" button.
- **Detail Sections:** For Phase 2/MVP, these can exist as distinct sections on a single long-scrolling Services page rather than separate sub-pages, to keep navigation simple and content dense.
